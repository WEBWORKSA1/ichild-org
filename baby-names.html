Tips for choosing a name

  • Say it out loud with your surname — rhythm matters. A short first name often pairs well with a long surname and vice versa.
  • Check the initials and common nicknames.
  • Think across languages if your family is multilingual — make sure the name is easy to say and has a positive meaning in each.
  • Sleep on it. Keep a shortlist for a few weeks and see which names you still love.
