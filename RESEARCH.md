# iChild.org — Niche research (38+ sites opened and reviewed, Nov 2026)

## Chosen concept
**iChild.org = a free child-development & parenting toolkit (pregnancy → age 12)** with interactive tools, live product-recall alerts, an auto-updating video library, research news and long-form guides.

Why this wins for "i child":
- Parenting/baby/family is among the highest-RPM AdSense verticals (baby gear, childcare, education, insurance, family finance advertisers).
- Tools (milestones, due date, sleep, childcare cost) create repeat visits and long sessions → more ad impressions.
- Every age stage creates a natural lead (email plan → partner referrals for childcare, tutors, therapists).
- The ".org" suits a trust-first resource, while staying an independent commercial publication.

## Key takeaways applied
1. Tools > articles for retention; articles > tools for AdSense approval — ship both.
2. Every trusted site has: medical disclaimer, editorial policy, privacy, plain language, accessibility.
3. Lead gen that converts: email-gated *personalized* deliverable + multi-step short form + service referral.
4. Donations convert with preset tiers, monthly option, designation choice and transparency.
5. Freshness: recall feeds and rotating tips make the site look alive daily (automated here via GitHub Actions).
