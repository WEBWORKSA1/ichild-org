Questions

How quickly will I get my plan?

Most plans go out within 24–48 hours. Check your promotions or spam folder if you don't see it.

Is it really free?

Yes. The plan and newsletter are free. iChild is supported by ads, partners and optional contributions.

Will you share my email?

We never sell personal data. If you opt in to partner offers, we only introduce you to relevant partners with your permission.
