Frequently asked

Is iChild.org free?

Yes. Every tool, checklist and guide is free. We are supported by advertising, partners and optional reader contributions.

Is my child's data stored?

Calculators and the milestone checker run in your browser. Checklist progress is saved only on your device. We only receive information you choose to send through a form.

Is this medical advice?

No. iChild.org provides general education drawn from public pediatric guidance. Always talk to your child's doctor about your child's health and development.

How often is the site updated?

Recall alerts, research headlines and the video library refresh automatically several times a day. Guides are reviewed regularly.

Who is iChild.org for?

Parents, grandparents, caregivers and educators of children from pregnancy to age 12. The site is written for adults and is not directed at children.
