💛 Our mission

About iChild.org

iChild.org helps parents and caregivers raise healthy, confident children with free, practical tools and plain-language guidance — from pregnancy to age 12.

What we do

Parenting information is everywhere, but it is often scattered, contradictory or buried under jargon. We bring the most useful pieces together in one fast, friendly place: interactive milestone checklists, calculators for everyday decisions, live product-recall alerts, an auto-updating video library and guides written for real life.

How we're different

  • Tools first. Answers you can act on in seconds.
  • Private by design. Calculators run in your browser; checklist progress stays on your device.
  • Always fresh. Recall alerts, research news and videos refresh automatically several times a day.
  • Grounded in public guidance. Our guides draw on recommendations from national health agencies and pediatric professional bodies. See our editorial policy.

Who we serve

Parents, expecting parents, grandparents, nannies, early-childhood educators and anyone caring for a child. iChild.org is written for adults and is not directed at children.

How we're funded

iChild.org is free to use and supported by advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labeled partnerships and optional reader contributions. Advertisers never influence our editorial guidance.

Independence

iChild.org is an independent publication. It is not affiliated with any government agency, hospital, charity or company referenced on the site, or with any business using a similar name. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.
