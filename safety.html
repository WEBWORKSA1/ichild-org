Safe sleep checklist

  • Baby on their back for every sleep
  • Firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet
  • Nothing else in the crib
  • Room-share without bed-sharing
  • No inclined sleepers or weighted sleep products

Babyproofing essentials

  • Anchor furniture and TVs to the wall
  • Lock away medicines, cleaners and button batteries
  • Gates at the top and bottom of stairs
  • Window guards and cord-free blinds
  • Smoke and CO alarms on every level

🌊

Water & outdoor safety

  • Arm's-reach supervision near any water
  • Four-sided pool fencing with a self-latching gate
  • Life jackets on boats and open water
  • Helmets for bikes, scooters and skates
  • Sun protection and hydration
