Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information iChild.org ("we", "us") collects, how we use it and your choices. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (free plan, referral request, partnership inquiry, donation pledge, contest entry, job application or contact form) we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, country, your child's age range and your message. We use this only to respond to you, deliver what you requested and — if you opted in — send our newsletter or introduce relevant partners.

Form submissions are transmitted securely through a third-party form-processing service to our private inbox.

Information that stays on your device

Our calculators and the milestone checker run entirely in your browser. Checklist progress, baby-name shortlists and your theme preference are stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to us. You can clear them anytime by clearing site data.

Children's privacy

iChild.org is a general-audience website directed at adults (parents and caregivers). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). Forms require users to confirm they are 18 or older. Contest entries must not include a child's face, full name or location. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies/local storage for site features, and advertising cookies as described above. See our cookie policy.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Embedded content behaves as if you visited the other website and is subject to its privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site (hosting, form processing, email), with partners when you have explicitly opted in, or when required by law.

Retention and security

We keep submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you gave them, then delete them. The site is served over HTTPS.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA and India's DPDP Act) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to withdraw consent or object to processing. To exercise these rights, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
