Cookie policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies and similar technologies (like local storage) are small files stored on your device that help websites work and remember preferences.

Essential

We use local storage to remember your theme, cookie choice, milestone progress and name shortlist. These never leave your device.

Advertising

Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Manage these at Google Ads Settings. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google requires consent through a certified consent management platform before personalized ads are shown.

Embedded media

YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only after you press play.

Your choices

Use the cookie banner, your browser settings, or the links above to control cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect site features.
