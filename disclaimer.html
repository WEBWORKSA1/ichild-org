Medical disclaimer

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Not medical advice

The information on iChild.org — including guides, milestone checklists, calculators, videos and news — is for general education only. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Always consult a professional

Always seek the advice of your pediatrician, family doctor, nurse or other qualified health provider with any questions about your child's health or development. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something you read on this site.

Emergencies

If you think your child may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number immediately. For a suspected poisoning, contact your regional poison control center.

Tools and calculators

Calculators give estimates based on general formulas and population guidance. Results may not apply to your child, especially for children born prematurely or with health conditions.

Third-party content

Videos, news headlines and recall notices come from third parties. We do not control or endorse their content; always check the original source.
