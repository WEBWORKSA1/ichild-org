    How to use this checklist

    Milestones are skills most children (roughly 3 out of 4) show by a certain age. They cover four areas: social and emotional, language and communication, thinking and problem-solving, and movement and physical development.

    If your baby was born prematurely, use their adjusted age (age minus the weeks born early) until about age 2.

    When to act early

    • Your child is missing several milestones for their age.
    • Your child loses skills they once had — at any age.
    • You have a gut feeling something isn't right.
    Early support works. If you have concerns, ask your child's doctor about a developmental screening, and ask about early-intervention services in your area — you usually don't need a diagnosis or referral to request an evaluation.

    Checklist items are written by iChild.org in plain language and informed by public developmental guidance from national health agencies. It is not a screening or diagnostic tool.
