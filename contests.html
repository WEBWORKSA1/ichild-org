Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase does not improve your chances.
  2. Open to parents/legal guardians aged 18+ where permitted by law. Void where prohibited.
  3. One entry per family per contest each month. Entries close on the last day of the month at 23:59 UTC.
  4. Little Artists and Parent Hack winners are chosen by a judging panel on creativity, originality and usefulness. Story Time winners are drawn at random from qualifying entries.
  5. Winners are notified by email and must respond within 14 days. Prize values and any sponsor are posted with each month's announcement.
  6. By entering, you grant iChild.org a non-exclusive license to display the entry on the site and newsletter with first name/initial only. You keep ownership.
  7. We never publish children's faces, full names or locations.
